Custom Bible Verse Widget: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your verses and settings stay on your device. To show some Bible translations, the app asks our server for the text; that request is not stored and isn't linked to you.

What stays on your device

Your saved verses and their formatting, your Proverbs and collection choices, your widget theme and other settings. The app and its widgets share them through an iOS App Group on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What leaves your device

  • The King James Version is built into the app and never needs the internet.
  • For the New King James Version and the New Living Translation, the app requests the chapter it needs from our server, which runs on Cloudflare. Our server gets that text from API.Bible, operated by the American Bible Society. API.Bible receives requests from our server, not from your device.
  • Like any internet request, ours includes your device's IP address and which book and chapter is needed. Our server uses that only to answer the request and to limit how many requests one address can make in a minute, and doesn't log or keep it. Cloudflare, which runs our server, handles requests under its own privacy policy. Our server keeps copies of Bible text, which contain nothing about you, for up to 23 days so it doesn't have to fetch the same chapter again.
  • To keep NKJV and NLT text current, as their license requires, the app and its widgets ask our server for the chapters you use again every few weeks, in the background.

Reminders

If you set up verse reminders, they're scheduled by the app on your device; nothing about them is sent to us.

What we don't do

We don't collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, device identifiers or usage data. We don't sell or share data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. Ask us to delete them at any time.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

We'll update this page and its date if anything changes.
