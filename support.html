Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add the widget to my home screen?

Long press on your home screen, tap the "+" button in the top corner, search for "Bible Verse", choose a widget size, and tap "Add Widget".

How do I add the widget to my lock screen?

Long press on your lock screen, tap "Customize", select the lock screen, tap the widget area below the time, then search for "Bible Verse" and add it.

Why isn't my widget updating?

The widget changes the verse every 5 minutes, every hour or once a day (Settings > Widget). iOS shows a schedule the app prepares in advance, so it works even when the app is closed. If the verses look stuck, open the app once; that refreshes the schedule. A pinned verse stays until it's unpinned.

Which Bible translations are available?

The King James Version (KJV), which works offline, and the New King James Version (NKJV) and New Living Translation (NLT). NKJV and NLT text comes from the internet and is refreshed every few weeks, as the license requires. After a week or more without an internet connection, some NKJV and NLT verses may show "Updating text…" until the app is opened online again.

What is Proverbs of the Day?

When enabled, the app automatically displays verses from the Proverbs chapter matching the current day of the month (e.g., Proverbs 19 on the 19th), mixed in with your other verses.

How do I set up verse reminders?

Open Settings, then Verse Reminders. Each reminder can show the verse on your widgets or a verse you choose, once a day or several times a day, on the days you pick. You can also tap Remind Me on any passage.

How do I pin a verse?

Open a verse, or press and hold it in the list, and choose Pin to Widgets: for today, or until you unpin it.

How do I send feedback?

Open the app, tap Settings, then Send feedback. It opens an email to BibleVerseWidgetApp@gmail.com that you can edit before sending.
